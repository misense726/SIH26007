# Mine terrain materials

`Diffuse.jpg` and `nor_gl.jpg` are the 1K JPEG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps
from Poly Haven's Aerial Rocks 02 asset, licensed CC0.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license
- Diffuse: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/aerial_rocks_02/aerial_rocks_02_diff_1k.jpg
- Normal: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/aerial_rocks_02/aerial_rocks_02_nor_gl_1k.jpg

The user's Bailadila layout image guided the scene composition. It is not bundled
or used as a background. Terrain elevation is illustrative, not survey data.
